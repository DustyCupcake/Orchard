Usage notes

  • Accent 1 drives primary buttons, active nav state, links and focus rings — filled, not just outlined.
  • Accent 2 marks a secondary emphasis only: a second badge family (shadowing, secondary series), never a second primary button.
  • Danger, warning and success are fixed across every community — they carry the same meaning everywhere and are never retinted to an accent.
  • Corners are sharp — 2px on small controls, 4px on cards and dialogs. No pill shapes, except a small round status dot.
  • Icons are Phosphor, Regular weight at 20px in grids and 16px inline. Fill weight, in Accent 1, marks the active/selected state only.
  • The community logo replaces the sidebar wordmark wherever it's set; unset, the sidebar falls back to the community's name in Inter 600.
  • Theme is personal, not a community setting: it follows each member's device (prefers-color-scheme) by default, with a light/dark override in their own profile.
  • Accent 1, Accent 2 and the logo are the community's — set once in Settings, and every theme (light or dark) resolves them through the same token names shown on this page.
